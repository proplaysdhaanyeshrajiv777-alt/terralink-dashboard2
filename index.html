      About the TerraLink prototype

      WHAT IS TERRALINK?

      TerraLink demonstrates how IoT technology can monitor underground environmental conditions and provide rapid warnings when predefined safety thresholds are exceeded.

      An Arduino Uno R4 WiFi reads the MQ-7 sensor, compares the reading to a threshold, and shows the status on the local 16×2 LCD and on this dashboard.

      HARDWARE

      Arduino Uno R4 WiFiMQ-7 gas sensor16×2 I2C LCDBuilt-in LED matrix

      ABOUT US

      We are Dhaanyesh and Arush, two students passionate about technology, innovation, and problem-solving. Together, we developed TerraLink, a project focused on exploring how the Internet of Things (IoT) can contribute to safer and smarter coal-mine environments.

      Our project combines technology and creativity to demonstrate how monitoring systems can help identify potential hazards and improve workplace safety. Through TerraLink, we aim to showcase the potential of IoT in addressing real-world challenges.

      This project represents our curiosity, teamwork, and commitment to learning, as we continue exploring new technologies and building innovative solutions for a safer future.

      This is an educational prototype and not a certified industrial mine-safety system.
      MQ-7 values are raw sensor readings, not calibrated gas concentrations, and are never shown as ppm.
